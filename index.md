# Duck Pond Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 October 2026

Duck Pond is a game made by Gawain Barnickle. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

## The short version

Duck Pond does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. We take none, and we keep none.

## What the game does not do

- It does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or any other personal details.
- It does not connect to the internet, so nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere.
- It has no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services that could gather data.
- It does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.
- It contains no in-app purchases.

## Your saved game

Duck Pond saves your progress (your ducks, decorations and hearts) in a file on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. That file never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and nobody else receives it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Children

Duck Pond collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the game needed any data at all, this page would say so before that version was released.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this repository: <https://github.com/gawainBarnickle/Privacy-policy/issues>
